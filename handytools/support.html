Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the tools?

Open any tool and tap the icon in the top right corner. It explains what the tool does and how to use it.

How do I unlock the other tools?

During onboarding, you pick one tool to keep free forever. To use the other tools, either watch a short rewarded ad to unlock them for the day, or unlock all tools permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

Can I change my free tool later?

Yes. Go to Settings and watch a rewarded ad to change which tool is your permanent free pick.

I bought the full unlock but it's not showing on this device. What do I do?

Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases." If the issue continues, email us below with your Apple ID email so we can look into it.

Why do I see ads?

Ads support the free tools in the app. Purchasing the full unlock removes ads and unlocks every tool permanently.
