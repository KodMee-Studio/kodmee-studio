KodMee Studio website: hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When pages are accessed, the host processes technically necessary access data (server log files), in particular IP address, date and time of access, page requested, referrer URL, and browser and operating system information.

The legal basis is Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure and stable operation of kodmee-studio.com. GitHub describes its international processing, retention criteria and transfer safeguards in its privacy statement.

KodMee Studio website: cookies and tracking

The corporate website at kodmee-studio.com sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking services. No fonts or scripts are loaded from external servers.

Contact

If you contact us by e-mail, we process your details solely to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b or lit. f GDPR. Data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for its purpose and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Access to your stored data (Art. 15)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21)
  • Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7 para. 3)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern.
