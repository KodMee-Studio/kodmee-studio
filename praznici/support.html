About Public Holidays

Browse public holidays and individual religious observances by calendar, year and language. Each published calendar includes official sources and the date its version was verified.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can read the public calendars directly in your browser.

How do I find my calendar?

Choose your country or region, then the year and a language available for that publication. Bosnia and Herzegovina has separate calendars for the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika Srpska and Brčko District.

Where can I check the dates?

Each published year lists its official sources and verification date. Dates marked “Estimated” still await confirmation.

Are individual religious observances days off for everyone?

No. They are listed separately from general holidays. The rules for taking religious leave depend on the relevant law and your employment arrangements; check the calendar's notes and sources.
